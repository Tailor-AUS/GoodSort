## What we do

Queensland's scheme pays 10¢. A large share of eligible containers still never reaches a refund point, because a depot trip is too far for most households.

Households scan eligible cans and bottles for a 5¢ sorting credit and sort into four streams at home. When suburb scanned volume is enough for one driver trip (about 1,000 containers), we tell households to bag out on the kerb. A runner collects and we deliver to an approved depot or refund point.

1. A household joins at thegoodsort.org (email, address, recycling day) and can scan the same day. That is not a live city-wide pickup.
2. We use Brisbane City Council open data to fill the recycling day for scheduling context. A volume run unlocks on **suburb container count**, not city-wide totals.
3. We collect sorted containers from the kerb in bags. We take them to a refund point or depot.
4. We receive the 10¢ scheme refund at the depot. The household gets a **5¢ sorting credit** (a private reward, not the scheme refund). Cash-out from $20 to an Australian bank.

Scan is the growth loop — photo verification is optional.
